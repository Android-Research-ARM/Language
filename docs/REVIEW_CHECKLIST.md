# Reviewer Checklist

This checklist is for maintainers and fluent community reviewers.

## Language review

- [ ] The translation preserves the meaning of the English text.
- [ ] The wording sounds natural to a fluent speaker.
- [ ] Spelling, grammar, punctuation, and formality are consistent.
- [ ] Menus and buttons are concise.
- [ ] Repeated terms use consistent translations.
- [ ] Delete, reset, and data-loss warnings remain explicit.
- [ ] Regional wording matches the language being reviewed.
- [ ] Text left in English is identified in the pull request.

## Safety review

- [ ] English text on the left was not changed.
- [ ] Markers such as `%@`, `%s`, `%ld`, `%.1f`, and `\n` remain intact.
- [ ] Product and technical names remain accurate.
- [ ] No advertising, links, personal information, or unrelated text was added.
- [ ] Machine-assisted text was checked by a fluent speaker.
