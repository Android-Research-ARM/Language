# Community Code of Conduct

The ARAS translation community welcomes contributors of every language, region, identity,
background, and experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Discuss wording and regional differences without dismissing other speakers.
- Give actionable feedback and assume good intent.
- Credit contributors and respect authorship.
- Protect personal information and report sensitive material privately.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, deliberate
misgendering, doxxing, spam, and knowingly malicious translations are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or reject contributions, remove comments, temporarily restrict
participation, or permanently ban participants when needed to protect the community.
Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the repository's
moderation or security contact. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This code applies in repository discussions, reviews, issues, and other spaces where a
person is representing the ARAS translation community.
